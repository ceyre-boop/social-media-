# Smiley — Gift Gallery v1

60 gifts, 1 to 10,000 blips.

---

## Currency

**100 blips = $1.00.** One blip = one cent.

One currency, one rate, published openly. No dollars → coins → diamonds. TikTok
runs a double conversion specifically so nobody can compute their own rate; making
ours a single sentence is a competitive feature, not a simplification.

| Blips | USD |
|---|---|
| 1 | $0.01 |
| 100 | $1.00 |
| 1,000 | $10.00 |
| 10,000 | $100.00 |

Creator receives 70% of post-fee value, itemized on every gift receipt.

**Bundle floor is $4.99** — Stripe's $0.30 fixed fee eats a third of a dollar
purchase. Bundles: $4.99 / $9.99 / $24.99 / $49.99 / $99.99.

---

## Design principle: gestures, not objects

TikTok's premium gifts are luxury objects — Lion, Motorcycle, Galaxy, Universe. A
gift that is an expensive *thing* makes gifting a flex, and the message becomes
"look what I can afford."

Smiley's gifts are **things you do for someone**. Scaling up doesn't buy a more
expensive object, it performs a bigger gesture. A Wave at 1 blip and a Parade at
1,200 are the same kind of thing at different volume.

Consequences for art direction and naming:

- **No luxury goods, no vehicles, no wealth signifiers.** Ever.
- Names are warm and human, never epic. "Standing Ovation," not "Supreme Glory."
- One coherent world: warm light, paper, weather, celebration, small kindnesses.
- Palette anchors to brand — yellow, cream, near-black — with each tier permitted a
  widening accent range.
- Icons read at 64px. Simple silhouette, one focal idea, no fine detail.

## Rules for future additions

1. **Never a luxury object.** No vehicles, jewelry, currency, or status goods. If
   the gift's appeal is what it costs, it doesn't belong.
2. **Every gift must be a gesture someone could actually make** for another person,
   literally or as a clear metaphor.
3. **Seasonal gifts are allowed, limited-time pressure is not.** A winter gift is
   fine. A countdown timer creating urgency to buy is not.
4. **Never gate a gift behind spend history.** No VIP-only tiers, no unlockables
   earned by past purchasing. That's the spending ladder, and it's the mechanic
   that makes fans feel graded by wallet.
5. **No gift may alter ranking, reach, or moderation.** Gifting is gratitude, not
   influence.

## Open items

- **Currency name.** "Blips" used throughout as a placeholder. Trademark-check
  whatever is chosen — the name appears in every price in the product.
- Animation specs per tier — separate pass once the gallery is locked.
- Icon production: 60 assets, one illustrator or a consistent generation pipeline.
  Consistency matters more than individual polish; TikTok's gift gallery looks
  incoherent because the assets came from everywhere.
